Duefy Account & Data Deletion

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Duefy provides users with the ability to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Duefy account and associated personal information, send an email to:

Email: zentroxstudioscare@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Duefy account. This helps us verify the account ownership and process the request securely.

2. What Information Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is successfully verified and processed, we may delete personal information associated with the account, including:

  • User account information
  • Email address associated with the account
  • User name
  • Subscription and reminder information stored in the account
  • Account-related application data

3. Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period when required by law, legal obligations, security requirements, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business purposes.

Such information will be retained only for the period reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

4. Google Sign-In Accounts

If you created or accessed your Duefy account using Google Sign-In, deleting your Duefy account will remove the Duefy account and data associated with it. It does not delete your Google account.

5. Subscriptions

Deleting your Duefy account does not automatically cancel any subscription or payment arrangement made through a third-party platform.

If applicable, users should manage or cancel subscriptions through the platform where the subscription was purchased.

6. Processing Time

We will review and process verified deletion requests within a reasonable period.

Additional information may be requested when necessary to verify the request and protect the security of the account.

7. Contact

For account deletion requests or questions regarding deletion of personal information, contact:

Email: zentroxstudioscare@gmail.com

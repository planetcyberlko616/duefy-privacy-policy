Privacy Policy for Duefy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Welcome to Duefy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Duefy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Duefy mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Duefy, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Email address, Google account information, and user name when you create or access an account.
  • Subscription and Reminder Information: Information about subscriptions, services, payment-related reminders, renewal dates, due dates, and other information that you choose to enter into the app.
  • Device Information: Certain technical information about your device, operating system, application version, and related identifiers may be collected for security, analytics, advertising, and crash reporting purposes.
  • Notification Information: Information necessary to send reminders and notifications that you have enabled within the application.
  • Usage Information: Information about how you interact with and use Duefy, such as app activity and feature usage.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Information: Technical information related to crashes, errors, and application performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your Duefy account.
  • To provide the features and services of the application.
  • To save and manage your subscription and reminder information.
  • To send reminders and notifications that you have requested or enabled.
  • To improve the performance, functionality, and user experience of Duefy.
  • To identify and fix application errors and crashes.
  • To understand application usage and improve our services.
  • To display advertisements through third-party advertising services.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the application.

3. Google Sign-In

Duefy may allow you to sign in using your Google account. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide information such as your name, email address, and basic account information to us for account authentication and management.

Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

4. Firebase Services

Duefy uses Google Firebase services to support certain application functions. Firebase may be used for authentication, data storage, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, and other application functionality.

Information processed through Firebase may include account information, application usage information, device information, diagnostic data, and other information required for the relevant Firebase service.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We may use analytics and crash reporting services, including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, to understand how users interact with the application and to identify technical problems.

These services may collect information such as device information, application version, operating system information, usage events, crash information, and diagnostic data.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

Duefy may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and information about interactions with advertisements, subject to applicable settings and laws.

Depending on your device settings, applicable laws, and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your device and Google account settings.

7. Notifications

Duefy may send notifications relating to reminders, subscription renewals, due dates, or other features that you enable.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

8. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Duefy. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access to information is limited to what is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the application.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or allow access to information with service providers that help us operate Duefy, including services used for authentication, cloud storage, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, and advertising.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, our services, or others.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce our agreements.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

11. Account and Data Deletion

If you wish to delete your Duefy account and associated personal data, please contact us using the email address provided below.

We may need to verify your request before processing an account or data deletion request.

12. Children's Privacy

Duefy is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through Duefy, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Duefy may use third-party services including Google services and Firebase. These third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples may include:

  • Google AdMob

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your information.
